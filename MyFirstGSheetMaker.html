G-Sheet Setup Guide

You must manually set your level on the Google Sheet

  1. Open your G-Sheet copy.
  2. Find the cell labeled CLASS(ES) & LEVEL(S) (Top Left).
  3. Type your class name followed by the number 5.
  4. Format: Wizard 5 or Fighter 5.
  5. Verify the LEVEL cell (Top Right) updates to 5.
