### Cartographic Dossier: The Hinterlands (The Frost-Marches)

#### I. Geography & The Climatic Anomaly

Nestled in a massive geographical pocket, the Hinterlands serves as the grueling, freezing transition zone between the realm's most extreme environments. To its North-East lies the explosive, humid overgrowth of the Greensea Expanse 1; to its North-West, the arid, time-locked "High Noon" of the Sands of Perdu 2, 6\.  
However, the climate of the Hinterlands is entirely dictated by what lies to its South: the Mighty Mountains 3\. This polar range was the domain of Colraneclast, The Glimmergale, the ancient Demiurge of Stasis 3, 7\. The sheer, residual cryonic magic pouring off her ancient peaks sweeps northward, locking the Hinterlands in a state of perpetual, punishing winter that acts as a physical barrier against the encroaching jungle and desert.

#### II. The Boreal Veil (The Cold Forests)

Acting as the immediate buffer against the Greensea and Perdu are the Cold Forests. This dense, sweeping taiga replaces the broad leaves and humid vines of the jungle with towering, needle-bearing evergreens and ancient, frost-coated Ironwoods.

* **The Silent Woods:** The dense snowpack and thick pine needles act as a natural acoustic dampener. The ambient noise of the realm drops to near zero here, creating an eerie, insulated silence broken only by the sharp *crack* of freezing bark or the howl of the wind.  
* **Fauna:** This is a territory of apex survivalists. Hungry Owlbears, packs of Winter Wolves, and massive, thick-furred beasts roam these woods 8\. The flora offers little sustenance, forcing the wildlife to be highly aggressive and fiercely territorial.

#### III. The Pale Tundra

North of the great lake, the tree line shatters entirely, giving way to the Pale Tundra. This is a vast, sweeping plain of pure permafrost and biting winds.

* **The Scars of the Long Chill:** During the period following the Old War known as "The Longest of Wintertide," this tundra was the site of desperate, freezing survival 9\. The permafrost here is so deep and magically resilient that no traditional crops can be grown; the earth is as hard as iron.  
* **Inhabitants:** The Tundra is the preferred roaming ground of the winter-adapted Semi-Gorgons. The **Jotun Rimebloods**, with their resistance to cold and their ability to sheathe themselves in the *Armor of Hoarfrost*, view this harsh expanse as their personal playground 10, 11\. The quieter **Aurora Callers** also meditate upon these plains, reading the shifting magical auroras that dance in the freezing sky 12, 13\.

#### IV. The Rime-Glass Lake

Separating the Tundra from the foothills of the Mighty Mountains is a colossal, frozen body of water.

* **The Frictionless Expanse:** The lake is frozen so deeply and with such pure cryonic magic that its surface is a sheet of perfectly reflective blue ice 5\. It functions as an environmental hazard; travelers without specialized spiked boots or magical means of traversal will find themselves slipping uncontrollably, spinning across the ice upon their backs with flailing limbs 5\. Agile rogues often "skate" across using daggers as rudders, while powerful sorcerers simply float above it to bypass the hazardous physics entirely 5\.  
* **Sub-Glacial Fishing:** Beneath the impenetrable ice, the waters are pitch black. Desperate foragers and ice-fishers cut deep holes to hunt for rare, cold-adapted aquatic life, though they risk drawing the ire of dormant, frozen Darkformes trapped in the depths.

#### V. Culture & Survival: The Thermal Protocol

To survive an expedition into the Hinterlands, a traveler must fundamentally alter their loadout. The altitude and perpetual storms create freezing conditions that turn minor inconveniences into lethal threats 4\.

* **The "Frozen" Hazard:** In the Hinterlands, standard or soiled protective garments are a death sentence. Extreme cold can cause a wet diaper to enter a "Frozen" state—freezing solid into a rigid block of ice that reduces a traveler's movement speed by 10 feet and imposes severe disadvantage on Stealth checks due to the loud, rhythmic crunching of the frozen absorbent 4\.  
* **Padding Perfection:** To combat this, explorers, rangers, and the native Jotun Rimebloods rely heavily on specialized magitek or the *Padding Perfection* spell. This transmutation magic ensures their heavy-duty, high-bulk variants (such as *All-Weather Shield* or *ArmoCotton 500*) remain "pleasantly warm," actively shielding the wearer from the biting chill and preventing the catastrophic freezing of their gear 4, 14\. In the Frost-March, a warm diaper is not a luxury; it is the literal difference between life and death.
