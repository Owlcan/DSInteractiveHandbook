# **Region Profile: The Sands of Perdu**

Designation: Temporal Hazard Zone / Historical Battlefield / Arid Wasteland  
Proximity: Edge of the Lissome Plains (Beyond the Tumbled Mountains Mordest)  
Threat Level: Severe (Environmental, Temporal, & Combat)  
Dominant Mana Type: Chronal / Entropic / Dry

## **I. History: Before the Eternal Day**

Before the **Iron March** and the subsequent "Eternal Day" that froze the sky, this region was known as the **Verdant Foothills of Mordest**.

It was the original home of the Academy’s **School of Psionics & Divination**. It was a lush, temperate zone characterized by floating quartz gardens, meditation lakes, and crystal-spire architecture designed to amplify mental prowess.

* **The Transformation:** The "Sand" of Perdu is not natural silica. It is the pulverized dust of those quartz towers and dried lakebeds, ground down by the sheer ontological pressure of the time-stop spell.  
* **The Eternal Day:** The sun in Perdu is pinned at a permanent "High Noon" position—the exact moment the spell was cast centuries ago. There is no night here, only a blinding, relentless white-gold exposure that bleaches color from equipment and drains the will of the living.

## **II. Key Locations & Hazards**

### **1\. The Dune-Sea of Pause**

The central expanse of the region. The dunes here shift not by wind, but by temporal ripples.

* **Hazard \- Chronal Pockets:** Stepping into the wrong depression in the sand can age equipment 100 years in a second, or freeze a traveler in place for a decade (perceived as a blink of an eye).  
* **Resource \- Chronal Crystals:** This is the primary source of the crystals sought by the **Caelistian Cults**. These shards are often remnants of the old Psionic focus-crystals, now infused with the concept of "stopped time."

### **2\. The Rust-Gardens**

A "forest" made of the twisted, protruding limbs of thousands of buried **Warforged**.

* **The Sleeper Host:** These constructs are not dead; they are *paused*. Loud noises or high-energy magic discharges can wake them.  
* **Scavenging:** Tech-savvy students and Artificers often brave this zone to harvest "Pre-Calamity" parts, though it is strictly forbidden by the Faculty.  
* **Terrain:** The ground here is metallic and jagged. It is essentially a landfill of ancient siege engines.

### **3\. The Tumbled Mountains Mordest**

Once a majestic range, now a field of floating boulders and shattered peaks that defy gravity due to the magical fallout.

* **Inhabitants:** The primary nesting grounds of the **Scorpion Peoples**. The caves here are actually hollowed-out sections of floating mountains, connected by massive silk bridges.

## **III. Time-Locked Enclaves (Echoes of the Past)**

Scattered throughout the wastes are "bubbles" of perfect preservation. These are areas where the time-stop spell worked *too* well, preserving the Old War in high-definition 3D.

### **1\. The Silent Amphitheater (The Psionic Lecture Hall)**

A massive, open-air lecture bowl carved into the side of a floating rock.

* **The Scene:** A lecture is permanently in progress. Hundreds of "ghosts" (actually temporal echoes) of students sit in rows, listening to a Psionics Master whose name has been lost to history.  
* **The Hazard:** If a living student sits in a seat, they become part of the lecture. They must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw to realize they are not a student from 700 years ago, or they will sit there "learning" until they starve.

### **2\. The Frozen Front (The Trenches)**

A mile-long scar in the earth where the **Iron March** first collided with Tabitha’s forces.

* **The Scene:** Mid-combat. Fireballs are frozen in the air like stained glass sculptures. Arrows hang motionless. Soldiers (both Academy defenders and Caelistian cultists) are statues of flesh and blood, trapped in the milliseconds before death.  
* **Interaction:** You can physically walk around a frozen explosion. However, touching a frozen soldier or spell breaks the time-tension, causing that specific 5-foot cube to "resolve" its action instantly (usually resulting in the spell exploding or the soldier dying).

### **3\. The Tea Terrace**

A small, heartbreakingly pristine cafe terrace that once overlooked the lakes.

* **The Scene:** A single table is set for tea. The steam rising from the teapot is solid, like sculpted cotton. A half-eaten scone sits on a plate. It is a moment of peace that was never finished.  
* **Lore:** It is rumored this was where **Galander Forsythe** had his last meal before marching to the front, though historians debate this.

## **IV. Inhabitants & Factions**

### **The Scorpion Peoples (The "Pokepadbutthoids")**

* **Origin:** Created \~770 years ago by Tabitha Caelestis using her own DNA and the **Great Weaver’s** bio-arcane schematics. Intended to be the ultimate desert infantry.  
* **The Betrayal:** Possessing the fierce individuality of the Weaver, they rejected Tabitha’s control and fled to the wastes.  
* **Culture:** They are survivalists and isolationists. They have a begrudging respect for the Academy (as the enemy of their enemy) but trust no one. They are heavily armored, naturally resilient, and wear customized protective padding made from woven cactus fibers and silk.

### **The Caelistian Cults (Interlopers)**

* **Objective:** Cells of the **Order of the White Hand** and student cultists frequently sneak into Perdu.  
* **Goal:** They seek **Prime Chronal Crystals**—shards that might contain the frozen memories or "whispers" of Tabitha Caelestis herself. They believe the Sands hold the key to resurrecting her or vindicating her "Dark Prime" philosophy.

### **The Hollowed Choir (The Ossuites)**

* **Origin:** A faction of calcified Darklings known as "Ossuites." Unlike fluid shadow-beasts, these entities have hardened into bone-like structures. They are born from the spiritual residue of the Old War's chaplains, paladins, and zealots who died screaming prayers into the void.  
* **Philosophy:** They worship the "Silence" of Perdu. They view the time-stop not as a curse, but as a holy preservation. They seek to "quiet" any intruders who bring the noise of the living world into their sacred crypts.  
* **Aesthetic:** They parody the religious orders of the Academy. They wear tattered vestments, carry bone instruments, and their "armor" often mimics the padding and heavy layering of the faithful, but constructed from ancient burial shrouds and hardened miasma.

## **V. Person of Interest: The Dust-Bound Sentinel**

Subject Name: Auraelie "Rattle" Vayne  
Classification: Darkling Ossuite (Mummified Sub-Type)  
Status: Wandering Combatant / Unaware Abomination  
Lore & Backstory:  
Aurelia was a promising graduate student specializing in Planar Containment during the Old War. When the timeline shattered, she was part of the forward unit holding the line. She never left.  
Over centuries, the entropic mana of Perdu replaced her biology. She is now an **Ossuite**—a creature of bone and shadow held together by sheer willpower and golden bands of hardened magical scar tissue.

* **The Tragedy:** Aurelia does not know the war is over. She believes she is still on the battlefield, merely "covered in soot." She perceives modern students as "fresh reinforcements" or "confused civilians" and will protect them with lethal efficiency.  
* **Appearance:** Her skin is bubbling black mana; her eyes are hollow, burning gold sockets. She wears the tattered remnants of a blue knit vest and tie, and a "heavy diaper" that is actually a containment vessel for the crushed dust of her enemies and the sands of time.  
* **Combat Behavior:** She wields a massive, chipped Greatsword inscribed with glowing runes. She apologizes for her "messy appearance" while cleaving constructs in half.

## **VI. Flora & Fauna (Bestiary)**

The biology of Perdu has adapted to the harsh, magical dryness, resulting in Entities that mimic the landscape (badly) and ancient constructs that have gone feral.

### **The Cactine Mimics**

Darklings in this region have attempted to evolve camouflage, but they fundamentally misunderstand the concept of "color."

* **Darkling-Cactine (The "Obvious" Ambush)**  
  * **Appearance:** A small, rotund Darkling that stands perfectly still with its arms raised in a "cactus pose." However, it is **jet black** and made of bubbling shadowstuff, making it painfully obvious against the beige sand.  
  * **Behavior:** It believes it is invisible. It will not move until you are within 5 feet.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Grab Backup:* If caught, it panics and summons reinforcements (1d2 rounds).  
    * *Prickly Jab:* Its skin has hardened into crystalline spikes.  
* **Darkling-Cactine-Biggo-Boy**  
  * **Appearance:** A larger, "Chonky" version of the Cactine. It resembles a barrel cactus made of void-matter.  
  * **Behavior:** Acts as a squad leader for the smaller Cactines.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Relentless Backup:* Constantly calls for help. Every round, there is a high chance (75%) of a standard Cactine appearing, or a lower chance (25%) of *another* Biggo-Boy arriving.  
    * *Painful Embrace:* A crushing hug that restrains the target.

### **The Serpentine Threats**

Localized variants of the standard Darkforme, evolved to burrow through the time-sand.

* **Darkspanner (The Siege Worm)**  
  * **Classification:** Huge Construct/Abomination Hybrid.  
  * **Appearance:** A massive, snake-like entity made of interlocking obsidian plates and solidified shadow.  
  * **Role:** An ancient siege-breaker unit from the Old War that has fused with the local fauna.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Siege Monster:* Tears through physical defenses and structures.  
    * *Crushing Coils:* Can constrict targets with immense force.  
    * *Tunneler:* Burrows through solid rock and time-glass, leaving a tunnel behind it.  
* **Darkforme-Spinebearer (The Wrestler)**  
  * **Appearance:** A reptilian or porcupine-like Darkling covered in jagged, bone-like quills.  
  * **Behavior:** Strangely obsessed with close-quarters grappling.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Heel ¡Lucha\!:* A signature move. When it grapples a target, it performs a devastating slam or crush maneuver, restraining the victim.  
    * *Spine Barrage:* Can launch quills from a distance if it cannot close the gap.

### **The Scavengers & Stalkers**

* **Nailbearer**  
  * **Classification:** Medium Construct (Feral).  
  * **Appearance:** A humanoid figure formed entirely from magnetic debris, rusted nails, and shrapnel from the **Rust-Gardens**.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Debris Camouflage:* Invisible when standing in junk piles.  
    * *Nail Burst:* Can detonate parts of its body to spray shrapnel in a cone.  
* **Lurker Primogenitor**  
  * **Classification:** Medium Abomination (Leader Type).  
  * **Role:** The "Alpha" of the stealth-based Darklings.  
  * **Abilities:**  
    * *Pack Coordination:* Telepathically commands other Darklings to move and attack out of turn.  
    * *Swallow Limb:* A terrifying grapple move where it attempts to engulf a victim's arm or leg in its shadow-maw, preventing the use of that limb for weapons or somatic spell components.

### **The Hollowed Choir (Ossuites)**

A specialized sub-species of "Ossokin" Darklings that mimic religious orders. They are fanatical, undead-like abominations (Unsettling Evil) that wander the crypts and ruins.

* **Ossokin Apostlyte (The Fanatic)**  
  * **Description:** A "weedy" skeletal warrior, resembling a stunted paladin in patchwork rib-cage plate. It is pathetic alone but dangerous in groups.  
  * **Key Mechanics:**  
    * *Aura of the Crypt:* Boosts nearby allies' saving throws.  
    * *Hollow Smite:* Can expend necrotic fervor to deal extra damage and Frighten humanoids.  
    * *Pack Tactics:* Fights better when near allies.  
* **Ossokin Declaryte (The Herald)**  
  * **Description:** A gaunt, robed figure acting as a bard/herald. It carries a scroll of skin and screams mocking verses.  
  * **Key Mechanics:**  
    * *Cutting Mockery:* Insults that deal psychic damage and disadvantage.  
    * *Liturgies of the Lost:* Chants to boost ally damage.  
    * *Spellcasting:* Uses bard spells like *Dissonant Whispers* and *Silence*.  
* **Ossokin Vestal (The Bratty Shrine Maiden)**  
  * **Description:** A parody of a shrine maiden wrapped in excessively thick, quilted white vestments. She wields a "broom" (bone trident) and channels high-voltage "divine brat energy."  
  * **Key Mechanics:**  
    * *Bratty Smite:* Deals massive psychic damage and knocks targets prone in humiliation.  
    * *Not Fair\!:* A reaction that deals psychic damage to anyone who dares hit her.  
    * *Padded Protection:* Resistance to non-magical bludgeoning.  
* **Ossokin Bonesinger (The Drone)**  
  * **Description:** A small skeletal figure that hums by rubbing its ribs together. It acts as a support unit.  
  * **Key Mechanics:**  
    * *Reassembling Song:* If dropped to 0 HP, it has a chance to reassemble at 1 HP unless hit by fire or radiant.  
    * *Hierarchical Pack Tactics:* Gains advantage only if fighting alongside a higher CR ally.  
* **Ossokin Bone Shaman (The High Priest)**  
  * **Description:** A morbidly obese skeleton—a paradox where solidified miasma fills the gaps to create a rotund silhouette. It is a powerful spellcaster.  
  * **Key Mechanics:**  
    * *Miasmic Belch:* Exhales a cone of necrotic smoke that poisons victims.  
    * *Lord of Bones:* Protects nearby undead from being Turned.  
    * *9th-Level Spellcaster:* Wields powerful cleric magic like *Spirit Guardians* (swirling bones) and *Contagion*.
