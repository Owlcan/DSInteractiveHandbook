# **REGION: MARISTANNIO (The Republic of the Iron Wake)**

**Designation:** Sovereign Maritime Republic / Magitek Naval Power

**Location:** The fortified coastal shelves and deep-water ports of the Bay of Blitz

**Atmosphere:** Salt spray, industrial smog, churning naval yards, and disciplined, pragmatic order masked by serene illusions.

# **HISTORICAL DOSSIER: THE GENESIS OF THE IRON WAKE**

**Designation:** Chronological Founding & Acquisition Record  
**Focus:** The evolution of displaced maritime nobility into the Admiralty Board and the multi-generational engineering of the Island-Dreadnought.

# I. The Shattered Fleet & The Flight Inland (\~701 Years Ago)

The progenitors of Maristannio were not the landed, bloodline-obsessed aristocrats typically found in Heraldia; they were the militarized maritime elite of the Western Coastal Theater. Before the Old War reached its zenith, these naval dynasties commanded the Academy's coastal defenses and maintained secondary strongholds near the opulent Day Manor on the Cliffs of the Nightwash.  
When magical artillery pulverized the western coastline approximately 701 years ago, these commanders faced total catastrophe. Their galleons were scuttled, their estates turned to molten glass, and the shores overrun by aquatic Darklings. Rejecting a suicidal march into the Lissome Plains, the surviving admirals and their artificer crews gathered the remnants of their fleet and retreated north, hunting for a defensible harbor.

# II. The Claiming of the Blitz Shelf (\~680 to \~600 Years Ago)

Amidst the freezing scarcity of the Long Chill, the surviving naval elite reached the storm-battered Bay of Blitz, initiating a brutal claim during the Great Land-Scramble.  
**The Shipwreck Foundation:** Maristannio was not built; it was salvaged. Finding a defensible deep-water shelf, the admirals intentionally grounded their battered warships into the mudflats. They utilized the massive, decaying wooden hulls as the skeletal architecture of their settlement, lashing the fleet together to forge the first bulwarks against the World-Seam monsoons.  
**The Eradication of Bloodlines:** During this grueling settlement period, traditional nobility perished. Realizing that ancestral titles offered no protection against a leaking hull, the lords formally dissolved their aristocratic houses. They established a pragmatic martial charter, transforming the noble families into the Admiralty Board—a meritocracy where engineering expertise and salvage tonnage superseded birthright.

# III. The Century of Obsessive Acquisition (\~600 to \~150 Years Ago)

With their borders fortified, the Admiralty Board succumbed to generational paranoia. Fearing the return of the Old War's apocalyptic violence, they began a centuries-long acquisition timeline to ensure they would never be caught unarmed again.  
**The Deep-Sea Dredging (\~550 Years Ago):** Artificers perfected hydro-arcane diving bells to scour the ruins of the Twilight Sea. For generations, they hauled up thousands of tons of Star-Iron ingots, unexploded magical munitions, and Robusca plating from the wrecks of their own former fleets.  
**The Rust-Garden Expeditions (\~400 Years Ago):** Clandestine teams conducted illegal, grueling treks to the Sands of Perdu. They plundered the Rust-Gardens, dragging the chassis of fallen Darkforged titans back to the Bay of Blitz to be melted in the coastal foundries.  
**The Hollowed Isle (\~300 to \~150 Years Ago):** The most classified engineering feat in their history. Utilizing hoarded Old War scrap, Carcharodonian welders and Ironwrights excavated the bedrock beneath the island. They replaced the stone foundations with interlocking gears, ballast-tanks, and geo-arcane engines, converting the sovereign territory into a dormant, stationary dreadnought.

# IV. The Silver Century Pivot (\~100 Years Ago to Present)

Approximately a century ago, the White Flag Concordat brought a regulated, bureaucratic peace. The Admiralty Board realized they had spent five hundred years constructing an impenetrable war engine for an apocalypse that had vanished.  
**The Arsenal of Commerce:** Rather than dismantling their failsafe, the Board made a brilliant pivot. They utilized ancient ballista bolts to pioneer explosive Abyssal Whaling and refitted siege engines to power the first armored "Turtle-Ship" merchant galleons.  
**The Bronze Canopy:** To mask the intense industrialization achieved over the last fifty years...

# I. Geography & Architecture: The Clockwork Canals

Situated on the most volatile, storm-battered coastal shelves of the Bay of Blitz, the sovereign island of Maristannio is a monument to industrial defiance wrapped in an illusion of absolute serenity. The city is a staggering fusion of intricate clockwork engineering, aquatic urban planning, and Far Eastern architectural elegance. 

**The Labyrinth of Water:** The city has no traditional streets. Instead, it is a "Clockwork Venice," a sprawling, intricate network of deep-water canals, locks, and hydro-arcane sluices. Citizens traverse the city on sleek, magitek-powered skiffs that glide silently over the dark water.

**The Iron-Pagodas:** The architecture marries heavy-duty naval fortification with sweeping, graceful Far Eastern aesthetics. The buildings feature sweeping, curved pagoda-style roofs tiled with overlapping scales of recast Star-Iron. Massive, imposing Torii-style gates forged from blackened steel mark the entrances to different merchant districts, and traditional sliding paper screens are replaced with glowing, frosted panels of reinforced Tower Glass.

**The Deep-Water Secret (The Island Dreadnought)**: To the outside world, Maristannio is simply a heavily fortified, stationary island. In truth, beneath the water line, the bedrock has been entirely hollowed out and replaced with colossal, interlocking gears, ballast-tanks, and massive geo-arcane propulsion engines. The Republic has essentially turned their entire sovereign landmass into a continent-sized dreadnought. Crucially, they have never once activated these engines for locomotion. It is a class-one state secret, a doomsday failsafe known only to the highest echelons of the Admiralty Board.

# II. The Illusion of Serenity: The Bronze Canopy

To live on top of a dormant, city-sized war engine presents a massive risk of discovery; the subtle, subterranean hum and vibration could easily alert foreign spies. To combat this, and to project an aura of effortless wealth to visiting merchants, the Admiralty Board commissioned the Bronze Canopy.

The Recycled War: After the Old War, the founders possessed mountains of Darkforged and Warforged scrap. Rather than simply melting it all into hull plating, master artificers meticulously forged the jagged, rusted limbs and chassis into thousands of artificial, bronze trees. 

The Magical Leaves: These metallic trees line every canal, bridge, and promenade, sculpted to resemble ancient, twisting bonsai and sprawling cherry blossoms. Their branches are adorned with thousands of magical, crystalline leaves that glow with soft, ethereal hues of neon pink, cyan, and warm gold. 

The Allure: The artificial canopy sheds a perpetual, beautiful twilight across the canals, masking the smog vents and muffling the deep, mechanical heartbeat of the island's hidden engines. It creates a breathtaking, melancholic illusion of a tranquil, Far Eastern water-garden, perfectly hiding the terrifying military reality beneath the citizens' feet.

# III. Origins: The Paranoia of the Twilight Sea

The founders of Maristannio were the pragmatic survivors of the Western Coastal Theater. During the climax of the Old War, these former naval commanders and artificers witnessed the absolute devastation of the Twilight Sea naval skirmishes, where indiscriminate magical artillery completely shattered the coastal dominance of the Academy.

The Great Stockpile: Traumatized by the loss of the Nightshores, the founders arrived in the Bay of Blitz during the Great Land-Scramble with one singular obsession: they would never be caught unarmed again. They obsessively hoarded Old War arms, stripping battlefields of Star-Iron ingots, Ancient Ballista Bolts, and volatile magitek artillery.

The Arsenal Pivot: As the Silver Century ushered in an era of heavily regulated peace, the Republic realized the apocalypse they were arming for had vanished. Sitting on a mountain of world-ending weaponry, they made a brilliant pivot, transforming their artillery into the engines of commerce that now power their island.

# IV. Industry & The Fleet: The Iron Wake

Today, Maristannio is the undisputed mercantile superpower of the western waters, their economy driven by extreme-risk salvage and maritime supremacy.

**The Turtle-Ship Dreadnoughts:** The stockpiled siege engines were refitted into maritime propulsion systems. Their merchant vessels are heavily armored magitek dreadnoughts styled after ancient "Geobukseon" (Turtle Ships), featuring fully enclosed, spiked iron roofs to shatter the monstrous waves of the World-Seam and repel boarding actions from aquatic Darkformes.

Abyssal Whaling: The ancient, heavy-ironwood ballista bolts from the Old War were re-engineered into explosive, deep-sea harpoons. The Maristannian fleets actively hunt the massive, corrupted fauna of the deep, such as Darkforme Titan-Snappers and Abyssal Leviathans, dragging their massive corpses back to the harbor to harvest their rare essences.

# V. Sociopolitical Structure: The Admiralty Board

Maristannio rejects the inflated, bloodline-based aristocracy of their neighbors in Heraldia entirely. The Republic operates as a strict, pragmatic meritocracy modeled after a naval chain of command.

The Admiralty Board: The city is governed by a council of Fleet Admirals, Salvage Barons, and Master Artificers. Political power is determined by the tonnage of one's merchant fleet and the profitability of one's salvage operations.

The Iron Law: Order is strictly maintained. The law is brutally efficient, treating citizens less like subjects and more like the crew of a massive warship. Mutiny, theft, and sabotage of the foundries are punishable by keelhauling or exile into the freezing mudflats.

# VI. Culture & Survival: The Ballast Protocol

Life traversing the slick, narrow clockwork bridges of the canals or working within the churning foundries demands survival gear that prioritizes absolute physical utility over magical elegance.

The Tar-Canvas Shells: Maristannian citizens and sailors utilize heavy-duty, stiff sail-canvas outer-shells treated with alchemical tar, Solvent, and Petrodistillate to completely seal out the freezing ocean spray and the dampness of the canals. 

The Ballast-Core: Because of the extreme kinetic vibrations of the island's hidden engines and the violent pitching of their dreadnoughts, Maristannian padding utilizes hyper-compressed Cotton Fluff integrated with heavy, alchemical absorbents. This design intentionally creates a remarkably dense, heavy garment. The added weight and extremely low center of gravity provided by a bulky, reinforced diaper is considered a vital tactical advantage, anchoring the citizen's stance as they balance on a speeding canal skiff or the slick deck of a storm-tossed warship.

# REGION: MARISTANNIO (The Republic of the Iron Wake) \- EXPANDED DOSSIER

Designation: Sovereign Maritime Republic / Magitek Naval Power

Location: The fortified coastal shelves and deep-water ports of the Bay of Blitz

Atmosphere: Salt spray, industrial smog, churning naval yards, and disciplined, pragmatic order masked by serene illusions.

## I. Demographics: The Citizens of the Wake

The Republic is a strict meritocracy. Bloodlines mean nothing; survival, salvage tonnage, and engineering prowess mean everything. The population is a melting pot of displaced artisans and aquatic survivors who perfectly complement the city's Clockwork Venice and Far Eastern aesthetics.

The Carcharodonians (The Deep-Hull Vanguard): The shark-folk are the undisputed masters of the Maristannian waters. The heavily-muscled Benthic Folk serve as the city's premier underwater welders, maintaining the classified, subterranean dreadnought engines and ballast tanks beneath the island. The agile Waves Folk and the tactically brilliant Cephalofoil Seers make up the elite officer class of the merchant fleets, utilizing their unparalleled sensory acuity to navigate the blinding storms of the World-Seam.

The Tanukijin & Foxfolk (The Clockwork Merchants): Seamlessly blending into the Far Eastern pagoda-architecture, the Tanukijin (Tanuki people) and Foxfolk (Vulpine Kin) manage the Republic's labyrinthine economy. Valuing adaptability and wit, they act as the city's primary diplomats, salvage brokers, and illusionists. They are largely responsible for maintaining the enchanting, neon-lit "Bronze Canopy" that masks the city's industrial smog in a serene, artificial twilight. 

The Ironwrights (Dwarves & Gnomes): Displaced during various historical purges and the "Technomantic Strike" of the Silver Century, enclaves of Dwarven and Gnomish artificers operate the roaring foundries of the lower docks. They work in tandem with the Carcharodonians, marrying deep-earth metallurgy with high-seas hydro-dynamics.

## II. Applied Technologies: The Arsenal of Commerce

Maristannio does not build for elegance; it builds for apocalyptic endurance. Their technology is a seamless, heavy-duty fusion of recycled Old War scrap and advanced clockwork.

The Turtle-Ship Dreadnoughts (Geobukseon): The pride of the Maristannian fleet. These massive galleons feature impenetrable hulls forged from melted-down Darkforged chassis and Star-Iron ingots. Their defining feature is a fully enclosed, curved roof covered in thick iron spikes. This design allows them to literally dive through the monstrous rogue waves of the Bay of Blitz and prevents boarding actions from Abyssal Leviathans and aquatic Darkformes.

Abyssal Whaling Harpoons: The ancient, heavy-ironwood ballista bolts salvaged from Old War battlefields have been re-engineered. Fired from pressurized steam-cannons, these harpoons are laced with conductive Fractal Copper, delivering a massive localized electrical shock to paralyze deep-sea targets for safe harvesting.

The Hydro-Arcane Sluices: The city's internal canal network is managed by massive clockwork locks cast from Robusca (a dense, crystalline alloy). These sluices use the kinetic energy of the incoming tides to power the city's internal foundries without burning excessive amounts of Petrodistillate.

## III. Naval Magics & Arcane Disciplines

The magic of the Republic is highly regulated, practical, and industrialized, sharply contrasting with the wild druidism of Niolantea or the pure wizardry of the Academy.

Luminancy & Structural Illusion: Practiced heavily by the Foxfolk and Tanukijin, this magic is weaponized to project an aura of effortless wealth. It powers the glowing, frosted panels of reinforced Tower Glass in the merchant districts and animates the soft, neon hues of the artificial bronze cherry blossoms that canopy the canals.

Abjuration (The Hull-Wards): The most respected magical discipline in the city. Abjurers are assigned to every dreadnought and Torii-style sea gate. Their sole job is to maintain the pressure-seals and kinetic barriers that keep the crushing depths and the World-Seam monsoons at bay. 

Hydro-Divination: Utilizing highly polished Defractor Prisms and Matrix Malachite, Maristannian "Storm-Seers" (often Cephalofoil Carcharodonians) scry the chaotic ocean currents. They do not predict the future; they predict the water, allowing the Republic to chart safe, temporary trade routes through the lethal Twilight Sea.

## IV. Notable Figures: The Admiralty Board

The Republic is governed by a council of Fleet Admirals, Salvage Barons, and Master Artificers. 

Grand Admiral Kaelen Deep-Maw: A towering, scarred Benthic Carcharodonian. He is the supreme commander of the Turtle-Ship fleets and the only individual outside of the Chief Engineer who holds the physical activation keys for the island's secret, subterranean dreadnought engines.

**Trade-Minister Yoko:** A brilliant, sharply-dressed Foxfolk who oversees the Silk-Road equivalents across the clockwork bridges. She commands a legion of spies and diplomats, ensuring Maristannio maintains its lucrative, monopoly-level trade agreements with the mainland while keeping their doomsday capabilities an absolute secret.

# I. The Philosophy of the Iron Wake

In Maristannio, padding is never an afterthought; it is a critical component of one's survival and social standing. Because the Republic is a strict meritocracy built on a churning, industrialized island-dreadnought, their diaper fashion completely rejects the frilly, pastel innocence of the central Academy or the delicate gold-spun silks of Heraldia. Maristannian fashion is defined by sleek metallic finishes, digital integration, waterproof utility, and heavy-duty clockwork aesthetics. 

## II. Working Class & Artificer Gear (The Ironwrights)

For the Dwarves, Gnomes, and Carcharodonian deep-hull welders working in the roaring foundries and subterranean engine rooms, durability is paramount.

\- RoboPads GearWorks: The absolute standard for the city's mechanics. These diapers feature a pattern of interlocking gears, cogs, and springs in shades of bronze, silver, and gunmetal grey. They utilize heavy-duty "Ratchet-Strap" style tapes and a durable, reinforced outer shell designed to survive sparks, grease, and heavy friction.

\- AquaPaws SplashZone: Heavily utilized by the Benthic Carcharodonians who perform external repairs on the seawalls. While playfully patterned with ocean creatures, its true value lies in its clear, water-resistant "Water-Seal" tapes and an outer shell designed to repel the freezing saltwater of the Twilight Sea.

## III. The Neon Aristocracy (The Merchants & Admiralty)

The Foxfolk and Tanukijin who manage the Republic's wealth and navigate the neon-lit, artificial twilight of the Bronze Canopy favor the absolute bleeding-edge of high-tech fashion.

\- PantiPulse Core: The crown jewel of Maristannian magitek fashion. This ultra-luxury diaper features a sleek, metallic finish integrated with digital tape controls and glowing biofeedback indicators. It represents the highest fusion of technology and fashion, worn by Fleet Admirals and wealthy Salvage Barons to project ultimate superiority.

\- Zenith Zendor: A cutting-edge, compact option boasting a deep metallic finish with subtle, glowing circuit-like designs. Its triple-tape system and refined, low-profile bulk make it the perfect engineered protection for a sleek, Far Eastern aesthetic.

## IV. The Shadow Fleet (The Smugglers & Scouts)

IV. The Shadow Fleet (The Smugglers & Scouts)

For the Cephalofoil Seers and privateers who navigate the deadly storms of the World-Seam and conduct illicit salvage operations in hostile waters, discretion is life.

\- StealthOps Covert Black & Nightshade Grey: Maristannio is the primary consumer of these tactical garments. The Covert Black offers a pure, matte black finish with noise-dampening outer layers and ultra-thin, super-strong black tapes. The Nightshade variant features a subtle carbon-fiber texture. Both offer maximum discretion and high-tech absorbent polymers for long, silent operations in the dark.

# RESOURCE DOSSIER: UNIQUE MARISTANNIAN EXPORTS & ITEMS

Designation: Industrial Alchemicals, Maritime Tools, and Arcane Salvage

Focus: Pragmatic, heavy-duty items designed for ocean survival and high-yield combat.

## I. Refined Materials & Munitions

While they hoard Star-Iron and Ancient Ballista Bolts for their dreadnoughts, the Maristannian foundries produce highly sought-after intermediate components for the rest of the plane.

\- Glimmerspark Wire (Copper Wire): The Republic utilizes massive spools of conductive Copper Wire. While used internally for their clockwork sluices and neon lighting, it is heavily exported to rangers and artificers across the realm to craft conductive Glimmerspark Arrows and specialized shock-weaponry.

\- Petrodistillate & Solvent: The foundational industrial liquids of the Maristannian economy. Harvested from the deep earth and refined in the coastal foundries, these highly volatile alchemicals are used to power their steam-engines, waterproof their heavy canvas clothing, and craft the plastic sheeting required for the realm's diaper manufacturing. 

## II. Maritime Tools & Arcane Instruments

The Republic produces specialized tools necessary for survival on the Twilight Sea and the volatile Bay of Blitz.

\- The Functional Good-Rod: A specialized, magically reinforced fishing rod highly prized by the Carcharodonian salvage fleets. It grants a magical \+1 bonus to all Fishing Checks, allowing sailors to effectively dredge up deep-sea salvage, lost cargo, or safely reel in hazardous aquatic Darklings from the churning waters.

\- Arcane Telescopes & Defractor Prisms: Crafted from polished Tower Glass and brass, these massive optical instruments are mounted in the city's pagodas and on the decks of their Turtle-Ships. Combined with Matrix Malachite, they are the primary tools used by Storm-Seers to practice Hydro-Divination and navigate the lethal rogue waves of the World-Seam.

## III. The Alchemical Stimulant

III. The Alchemical Stimulant

\- The "Can of Whoopass": A crude, legendary, and highly volatile industrial consumable favored by the grueling dockworkers, ironwrights, and dreadnought marines of Maristannio. Packaged in a pressurized tin cylinder, consuming this potent mixture grants the drinker a \+1 bonus to Attack and Damage rolls for 1d4 hours. However, the sheer biological shock of the heavy stimulant instantly forces the drinker to make a sudden, disadvantageous Potty Check, making it a favorite among those who rely on the city's heavy-duty padding to get through a grueling, dangerous shift.
