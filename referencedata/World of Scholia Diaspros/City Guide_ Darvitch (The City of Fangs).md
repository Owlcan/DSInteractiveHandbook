# **City Guide: Darvitch (The City of Fangs)**

Population: \~11 Million  
Primary Exports: Magitek, Advanced Textiles (Diapers), Automata, Refined Shadow-Mana  
Ruler: The Council of Mothers (The Mothers of Night)  
Law Enforcement: The Iron-Guard (Automaton Police) & The Unseen Hand (Shadow-Enforcers)

## **I. Urban Layout: The Vertical Caste**

Darvitch is not measured in "North" or "South," but in "Altitude." Gravity and sunlight (or the lack thereof) define your station.

### **1\. The Upper Fangs (The Crown)**

Inhabitants: The Pampire Aristocracy, High-Ranking Nightweavers, Wealthy Magitek Magnates.  
Atmosphere: Silent, sterile, and opulent.  
Suspended by gravity-inversion rituals, these crystalline towers hang upside down from the sky. The streets here are made of transparent aluminum, allowing residents to look down upon the smog of the lower city.

* **The Moon-Bridge:** The central thoroughfare connecting the towers, made of solid moonlight.  
* **The Night Gardens:** Bioluminescent botanical parks where the elite socialize.  
* **The Sanguine Salons:** High-society clubs where "Vintage Blood" (aged in enchanted casks) is sipped from crystal flutes.

### **2\. The Mid-Fangs (The Mesh)**

Inhabitants: Merchants, Artificers, The Middle Class, Gnomes.  
Atmosphere: Neon-lit, busy, commercial.  
This is the area where the massive Nightweaver Silk Cables connect the floating upper city to the ground. It is a suspended district of platforms, cable-cars, and hanging markets.

* **The Silk-Road:** A massive suspension bridge marketplace where the famous Darvitch textiles are sold.  
* **Tech-Row:** The hub of artificers. If you want a clockwork limb or a self-cleaning diaper, you come here.

### **3\. The Lower Fangs (The Grime)**

Inhabitants: Laborers, Low-Ranking Drow, The Poor.  
Atmosphere: Industrial, smoggy, loud.  
The ground level is a dense jungle of obsidian skyscrapers, steam vents, and geothermal power plants. The sun never touches these streets; they are lit by flickering gas-lamps and neon signs.

* **The Foundries:** Massive factories that run 24/7.  
* **The Sump:** The docks at the water's edge, where the black water of the Twilight Sea meets the city's waste.

## **II. Industry: The Magitek & Textile Boom**

Darvitch's economy relies on two things: **Artifice** and **Padding**. Because of the Pampire population's unique biology (high-stress metabolism requiring protection) and the Nightweavers' silk, Darvitch produces the most advanced undergarments on the plane.

### **Famous Darvitch Exports (The Brands)**

The factories in the Lower Fangs produce these famous brands found in the *Combat Academy Handbook*:

* **StealthWear (The Assassin's Choice):**  
  * *Origin:* Manufactured by **The Unseen Hand's** front companies in the Mid-Fangs.  
  * *Tech:* Uses sound-dampening shadow-weave silk.  
  * *Market:* Popular among rogues, spies, and nobles who value discretion above all else.  
  * *Lore:* "If you can hear it crinkle, it's a knock-off."  
* **TactiTush (Military Grade):**  
  * *Origin:* Produced by the **Iron-Foundries** for the city guard and mercenaries.  
  * *Tech:* Reinforced with ballistic weave and impact gel.  
  * *Market:* Adventurers and front-line fighters.  
* **DiaperDynamo (The Artificer's Special):**  
  * *Origin:* Gnomish innovation from Tech-Row.  
  * *Tech:* Contains actual mana-capacitors.  
  * *Lore:* Often used to power small devices or store spare spell slots (represented by use charges).

## **III. Factions & Society**

### **1\. The Mothers of Night (The Council)**

The supreme authority. A coven of ancient Pampire Matriarchs who haven't touched the ground in centuries.

* **Current Head:** **Matriarch Vespera** (Descendant of Valerica). She is rarely seen, communicating via a massive holographic projection over the Moon-Bridge.

### **2\. The Nursery Nightweavers (The Architects)**

These giant, intelligent arachnids are co-rulers, not pets. They maintain the structural integrity of the city.

* **The Weaver's Guild:** They control the price of silk. Crossing them is a death sentence; they can literally deconstruct your house while you sleep.  
* **Fashion Role:** A Nightweaver tailor can spin a custom suit (or diaper) *around* a client in seconds, fitting it perfectly to their body.

### **3\. The Unseen Hand (The Corporate Guild)**

In Darvitch, the Thieves' Guild is a registered corporation.

* **Headquarters:** **The Velvet Spire** in the Mid-Fangs.  
* **Operations:** They handle "High-Risk Logistics." Smuggling, assassination, and corporate espionage are menu items with set prices. They ensure that while crime happens, it doesn't disrupt *business*.

## **IV. Culture: The "High Goth-Tech" Aesthetic**

Darvitch has a unique cultural identity known as **"The Masquerade."**

* **The Look:** Leather, lace, neon accents, and visible, high-tech padding. Because the aristocracy wears diapers openly (often decorated with jewels or glowing mana-lines), it has become a fashion statement for the lower classes to emulate.  
* **Time:** There is no day or night. The city runs on three 8-hour "Shifts." The bells of the **Clockwork Cathedral** ring to signal shift changes.  
* **Entertainment:** The **Opera of Shadows** features Banshee singers who can weep melodies that induce magical emotions in the audience.

## **V. Key Locations for Adventurers**

### **The Clockwork Cathedral**

A massive temple to the God of Time and Industry (an aspect of the Great Weaver). It houses the **Atomic Chronometer**, the device that keeps time for the entire city in the absence of the sun.

### **Club Midnight**

The most exclusive club in the Upper Fangs. The floor is made of glass, looking down 2,000 feet to the city below. It is the best place to meet a Mother of Night—or get eaten by one.

### **The Scrapyard (The Rust-Bites)**

A dangerous zone in the Lower Fangs where failed automata and rejected magitek are dumped. It is inhabited by **"Junk-Druids"** (Gnomes who bond with trash) and feral constructs.

# **The Chronicles of Night: The Founding of Darvitch**

**Era:** The Middle-Expansion Period (Approx. 600 Years Ago) **Subject:** The Exodus of the Sun-Scorned, The Pact of Silk, and The Rise of Magitek **Cross-Reference:** The Great Weaver, Horace Hootz (The Founding Era), The Unseen Hand

## **I. The Exodus of the Sun-Scorned**

Six hundred years ago, the Plane of Scholia Diaspros was politically dominated by the growing influence of the Academy in the east and the wild, magical experimentation of Elzbereth. For the **Pampire** bloodlines—ancient, aristocratic vampires afflicted with a unique curse of regression and high-stress metabolism—life in the sunlit lands was a gilded cage.

While not hunted to extinction, they were social pariahs, forced to hide their dependency on "protective garments" (diapers) and their aversion to sunlight. They lived in sealed basements and blacked-out carriages, ruling only in the brief hours of true night.

**The First Mother: Valerica Von Vesper** The movement began with **Valerica Von Vesper**, a matriarch of immense age and power. Tired of the "Day-Tyranny," she rallied the scattered noble families—the **Mothers of Night**. Her proclamation was simple:

*"We shall not live in the shadow of the sun, hiding our comforts and our nature. We shall find a place where the sun dares not tread, and there, we shall build a throne."*

They gathered their wealth, their thralls, and their vast libraries of forbidden lore, embarking on a massive westward caravan known as **The Black Procession**.

## **II. The Twilight Discovery**

The Black Procession crossed the **Sands of Perdu** by night, burying themselves in the sand during the scorching days. Many perished, but Valerica pressed them onward, guided by visions from a deity she called **The Dark Nurse** (later revealed to be an aspect of the Great Weaver).

When they finally breached the western edge of the desert, they did not find an ocean, but the **Amber Savannah**. They watched as the sun dipped to the horizon and simply... stopped.

They had found the **Nightshores**.

It was a geological and magical anomaly—a "planar tilt" where the world curved away from the metaphysical sun. As they pushed further west to the **Isle of Evening**, they found a land of eternal starlight, warm thermal vents, and endless biological richness. It was a paradise where a vampire could walk "abroad by day" because "day" no longer existed.

## **III. The Pact of Silk (The Nightweaver Alliance)**

The Isle of Evening was not uninhabited. It was the nesting ground of the **Nursery Nightweavers**, a divergent sub-species of Diaparachnids who had separated from the Great Weaver's main brood centuries prior.

These arachnids were colossal, intelligent, and fiercely territorial. They had woven massive, sticky fortresses between the island's volcanic spires.

**The Negotiation** Instead of war, Valerica Von Vesper offered a trade. The Pampires brought something the Nightweavers desperately lacked: **refined mana** and **cattle** (in the form of willing thralls and livestock). In exchange, the Nightweavers offered their silk—stronger than steel, capable of conducting magical energy—and their labor.

This alliance was sealed as **The Pact of Silk**.

* The Pampires were granted the high ground (the spires).  
* The Nightweavers were granted the under-city and the right to weave the city's infrastructure.  
* The two species would co-exist as the ruling elite, protecting each other from the sun-lovers of the East.

## **IV. The Raising of the Fangs**

With the labor force of thousands of giant spiders and the telekinetic might of the vampiric covens, construction was impossibly fast.

**The Architecture of Necessity** Because they rejected the solar-based magic that powered much of the Academy's agriculture and heating at the time, the founders of Darvitch turned to **Geothermal** and **Arcane-Industrial** power.

* They tapped into the volcanic vents of the Twilight Sea, building massive steam-turbines.  
* The Nightweavers spun **Mana-Cables** from conductive silk to transport this energy.

This necessity birthed the **Lower Fangs**—the industrial heart of the city. To rise above the smog and noise, the Mothers of Night used gravity-inverting rituals to raise the **Upper Fangs**, the floating crystalline towers that serve as the noble districts today.

## **V. Key Historical Events**

### **The Ruin of Day Manor (Pre-Founding Context)**

Before Darvitch rose, the Nightshores held only one significant structure: **Day Manor**. It was a hedonistic retreat built by Academy mages *before* the Old War, designed to view the eternal sunset.

* **The Event:** During the climax of the Old War (approx. 710 years ago), a naval skirmish between rival mage-factions led to the shelling of the coast. Day Manor was shattered.  
* **Significance:** When the Vampires arrived 100 years later, they found the manor infested with Darklings. They chose *not* to rebuild it, leaving it as a "monument to the folly of the Day-Dwellers." It remains a dangerous ruin to this day.

### **The Gear-Turn Migration (400 Years Ago)**

As the Academy entered its deepest period of stagnation and anti-technological sentiment, the Gnome clans of the East faced persecution. Their inventions were deemed "soulless" and "unstable."

* **The Event:** A mass exodus of Gnomish Artificers fled the Academy and crossed the Sands of Perdu to Darvitch.  
* **The Integration:** The Mothers of Night, seeing the utility of engineering, welcomed them. This fused Gnomish clockwork genius with Nightweaver Silk and Vampiric Blood Magic, birth the unique "Magitek" aesthetic of the city.

### **The Neon Revolution (150 Years Ago)**

For centuries, Darvitch was powered by steam and gas.

* **The Discovery:** Alchemists discovered that Nightweaver silk, when treated with specific bioluminescent fungi from the Amber Savannah, could act as a lossless conduit for pure mana.  
* **The Shift:** The city replaced gas lamps with "Mana-Neon." This transformed the skyline from a dark, gothic industrial zone into the glowing, cyberpunk metropolis seen today. It also allowed for the miniaturization of technology, leading to the creation of **Use-Charge Diapers** (like *StealthWear* and *DiaperDynamo*), which require mana-microcircuits to function.

## **VI. The Shadow-Economy (The Unseen Hand)**

As Darvitch grew, it became the envy of the world. But it was isolated. To move their goods (and obtain fresh blood/resources), the Mothers of Night formalized their relationship with the criminal underworld.

They sanctioned **The Unseen Hand** as an official guild within the city—the only place on the plane where the thieves' guild operates openly as a corporate entity. The Hand became the logistics arm of Darvitch, smuggling Magitek out to the world and bringing wealth back in.

## **VII. The Era of Silence: The Great Divergence (600 \- 200 Years Ago)**

The defining moment for Darvitch's global dominance came not from its own actions, but from the fall of the Academy.

Following the **Old War** (approx. 710 years ago) and the devastation caused by Tabitha Caelestis, the Academy entered a period known as **The Great Silence**. The East closed its borders, banned "Dangerous Arcana" (Necromancy, Shadow-Weaving, Artifice), and turned inward.

**The Vacuum of Power** While the Academy slept, Darvitch awoke.

* **The Refugee Influx:** Necromancers, Shadow-Mages, and aggressive Artificers fled the Academy's purges and came to the Nightshores. Darvitch absorbed this "brain drain," gaining centuries of magical knowledge that the Academy had tried to bury.  
* **The Mono-Market:** For nearly 400 years, Darvitch was the *only* functioning, open-market metropolis on the plane. If a kingdom needed magical steel, or a wizard needed a focusing crystal, they had to trade with the Mothers of Night.

**The Technological Split** This era solidified the cultural divide between East and West.

* The Academy remained frozen in "Pure Magic" (classic wizardry).  
* Darvitch advanced into "Magitek" (industrial magic).

**The Cold Peace** By the time the Academy reopened its borders in recent centuries, Darvitch was too powerful to conquer. A "Cold Peace" was established. The Academy ignores Darvitch's "heretical" technology and vampire leadership, and in exchange, Darvitch does not openly invade the sunlit lands—though their economic spies are everywhere.

## **VIII. Modern Darvitch**

Today, Darvitch stands as a testament to **The Alternative Path**. It is a city that proved one does not need the light to shine. It is a place of neon darkness, where ancient monsters wear the finest silk, and the hum of machinery replaces the song of birds.

**The Mothers of Night** still rule, eternal and unaging in their floating gardens, watching over a city that is half-factory, half-palace, and entirely nocturnal.
