### Region Profile: Gildermount (The Surface Keystone)

**Designation:** Geothermal Alpine Gateway / Sovereign Surface Settlement**Location:** Situated directly north of the Nightshores, resting upon the lower slopes of the Dracespire Mountains.**Atmosphere:** A striking juxtaposition of crisp alpine air, heavy sulfurous mists, and the constant, comforting rumble of deep-earth geothermal activity.**Key Phenomenon:** The Gilded Mount (The Active Mini-Caldera)

#### I. Geography & The Gilded Mount

If the higher peaks of the Dracespire Mountains are defined by their punishing, absolute-zero blizzards and razor-sharp ice 4, the lower slopes of Gildermount represent a miraculous pocket of geothermal salvation.  
Here, the ontological dysfunction and planar transversality that plague other regions completely burn away, neutralized by the sheer, grounding heat of the earth. The centerpiece of this region is **The Gilded Mount**, a lonely, massive, and semi-active mountain volcano. While it does not erupt with apocalyptic force, its peak cradles a boiling mini-caldera. This constant geological activity creates a vast network of cascading hot springs, bubbling mud pots, and steaming geysers that terrrace down the mountainside, painting the rocky slopes in vibrant streaks of copper, sulfur-yellow, and deep rust.

#### II. The City of Gildermount (The Threshold of Elzbereth)

Before one can descend into the suffocating, mechanized depths of Elzbereth, one must pass through its literal "roof." Gildermount is a bustling, heavily fortified dwarven surface city built directly into the terraced slopes of the Gilded Mount.

* **The Architecture:** The city is a masterpiece of pragmatic, high-altitude engineering. It features heavy stone bunkers, massive brass ventilation pipes venting steam from the city below, and sprawling, open-air trading plazas.  
* **The Keystone Gates:** Gildermount serves as one of the three major access routes into Elzbereth. The center of the city is dominated by a colossal, heavily guarded set of adamantine vault doors that lead down into the subterranean metropolis 3\.  
* **The Economy:** Because Elzbereth itself can be claustrophobic and overwhelming for non-dwarven merchants, Gildermount acts as the primary surface trading hub. Caravans from the Greensea and the Nightshores offload their goods here, while weary travelers eagerly pay premium prices to soak their aching bodies (and their soiled, heavy-duty padding) in the highly restorative, mineral-rich hot springs that border the city.

### III. New Radiana (The Phoenix Township)

Resting on a fertile, geothermally warmed plateau just below the dwarven gates of Gildermount—and cascading elegantly down toward the coastal waters—is the poignant and thriving township of **New Radiana**.  
Born from the ashes of Lesser Radiana, which was entirely consumed by etheric nuclear flame during the Old War 1, 2, this settlement has far surpassed its ancestral roots as a mere agricultural breadbasket. Founded around the same era as the rebuilding of Majicka, New Radiana has evolved into a breathtaking, sovereign magitek township that rivals the Academy's primary neighboring city in both wealth and innovation.

#### A. The Magitek Infrastructure: Air and Sea

While Majicka is heavily defined by its proximity to the campus proper and relies primarily on the magically maintained cobblestones of the Road to Majica 3, 4 alongside basic, low-altitude air travel, New Radiana boasts a vastly superior, multi-tiered logistical infrastructure.

* **The Geothermal Seaport:** Because the township's lower districts reach the warm, coastal waters at the edge of the Nightshores, New Radiana operates a massive, ice-free magitek seaport. Steam-powered galleons and magically propelled cargo freighters dock here year-round, bypassing the treacherous terrestrial routes to directly trade raw volcanic materials and exotic greenhouse yields.  
* **The Thermal Air-Docks:** Utilizing the powerful, constant thermal updrafts generated by the Gilded Mount's mini-caldera, New Radiana features a sprawling, high-altitude airship marina. Massive dirigibles, sky-galleons, and individual soaring-skiffs effortlessly ride the hot air currents up to the city's highest plateaus, giving the township an unparalleled vertical mobility that Majicka sorely lacks.  
* **The Architecture:** The city is a beautiful blend of sleek, modern magitek engineering and natural thermal integration. Streets are heated from below by piped hot spring water, preventing any ice from forming, and the skyline is a mix of glowing glass greenhouses, brass clockwork transit lines, and beautiful residential spires draped in flowering, heat-loving vines.

#### B. A Tapestry of Survivors (The Diverse Demographics)

Because New Radiana represents survival and rebirth, it is one of the most wildly diverse melting pots in the entire Scholia Diaspros demiplane. It lacks the rigid, aristocratic purity testing of Heraldia and the dark, vampiric exclusivity of Darvitch's upper fangs.  
Here, dwarven engineers from Gildermount work alongside Bee Crinkly-Crawlie botanists who manage the massive greenhouse complexes 6, 7\. Carcharodonian dockworkers manage the shipping lanes of the seaport 8, while elementalists and artificers walk the neon-lit promenades. The culture of New Radiana is deeply communal, defined by a shared appreciation for the second chances the city represents.

#### C. The Alumni Haven (The Comfort of the Known)

Perhaps the most defining cultural aspect of New Radiana is its status as the ultimate retirement sanctuary for the Academy's esteemed Alumni 5\.  
When a student finally graduates from the Scholia Diaspros, they face a difficult choice. Many find that they truly cannot bring themselves to leave the safety, the padding, and the magical convenience of the demiplane. However, the prospect of settling in Majicka—with its endless influx of dramatic first-years, constant proximity to the Academy's chaotic "Living World" events, and tiring school politics—feels far too typical and exhausting for a seasoned veteran of the realm.  
New Radiana offers the perfect solution: **Something fresh, but known.**

* **The Perfect Distance:** It is far enough away from the inner campus to be insulated from the daily explosions and Darkling incursions, offering genuine peace and quiet. Yet, it remains deeply connected to the realm's magitek grid and plush amenities.  
* **The Padded Lifestyle:** The township caters heavily to the luxurious, diapered needs of its alumni. High-end boutiques sell premium, thermal-regulated padding perfect for the hot springs, and the residential manors are built with all the self-cleaning, magically softened furniture an exhausted former hero could ever desire.

How flawlessly does this elevate New Radiana from a simple farming village into a bustling, sophisticated jewel of the coast, Master Archivist? With its airships catching the thermal drafts and its alumni enjoying their hard-earned rest in the hot springs, it stands as the ultimate reward for surviving the Academy\!
