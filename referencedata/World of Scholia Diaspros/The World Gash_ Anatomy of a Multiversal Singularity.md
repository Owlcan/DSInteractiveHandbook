### Region Profile: The World Gash (The Maw of the North-East)

**Designation:** Absolute Dimensional Fissure / Hostile Incursion Graveyard**Location:** A massive planar split jutting "outward" from the northernmost expanse of the Easterseam, forming the jagged "nose" of the Dracoseam's edge.**Atmosphere:** Deafening, gravity-warped, and choking on a perpetual maelstrom of descending cosmic detritus, howling void-winds, and the shrieking, twisting metal of crushed armadas.**Key Phenomenon:** The Terminal Descent (The One-Way Funnel)

#### I. The Geography of the Tear

If the Great Spill of the Dracoseam is a clean, terrifying drop-off where the ocean falls into the starry void of The Ways 6, the World Gash is a catastrophic structural failure of the demiplane itself. Situated at the extreme northern "nose" of the eastern boundary, the planar membrane here has not just frayed; it has split open in a massive, jagged, three-dimensional canyon of raw space-time.  
The World Gash is uniquely defined by its horrific, localized gravity. It is a chaotic place from which many things descend, but almost nothing ever emerges. The kinetic friction of the Academy's reality scraping against the absolute void 2 has created a localized singularity—a multiversal funnel. Rather than water spilling *out*, the sky and sea are violently churning as an endless, terrifying torrent of cosmic debris, violent weather systems, and displaced entities are sucked *down* from the higher Ways and violently expelled into the churning waters of the northern Eastersea.

#### II. The Folly of the Dark Dimensions

The World Gash represents an incredibly tempting, yet perpetually fatal, strategic prize for the rival, militarized academies of the multiverse. The **Dark Dimensions** are known for their rigid, brutal efficiency and their heavily armored "Incursion Teams" comprised of Trainee Auditors and Project Auditors who seek to capture and catalog the chaotic biology of our realm 4, 5\.  
Viewing the massive, one-way funnel of the World Gash as the ultimate rapid-deployment beachhead into the Scholia Diaspros, the Dark Dimensions continuously attempt to navigate massive, heavily fortified invasion flotillas down through the tear.  
However, they fundamentally fail to understand the nature of the Gash. The Dark Dimension forces rely on absolute, unyielding order—perfect geometric dreadnoughts of black steel and rigid magical shielding. But the World Gash is a tempest of pure, unadulterated planar chaos. As their flawless, obsidian flotillas descend into the "nose" of the Seam, the sudden, violent fluctuations in gravity, the temporal sheer, and the crushing kinetic pressure of the dimensional walls warp their ships beyond recognition.  
The result is a spectacular, horrific graveyard. The dark flotillas do not sail out of the Gash; they are invariably crumpled into massive, burning balls of twisted iron and screeching auditors, violently ejected from the bottom of the rift to crash into the boiling seas below. The attempts are nearly always unsuccessful, leaving the waters choked with the wreckage of a thousand failed invasions.

#### III. The Ecology of the Descent

Because the World Gash acts as a cosmic drainpipe, the waters surrounding its base are some of the most dangerous and bizarre in the entire Scholia demesne.

* **The Rain of Chaos:** The Gash perpetually "vomits" matter into the sea. At any given moment, one might witness a shower of highly valuable "Plastic Drift" 7, chunks of dead, alien planets, or the writhing, confused forms of extra-dimensional horrors that were sucked into the funnel from realities millions of lightyears away.  
* **The Wreckage Reefs:** The crushed, half-sunken dreadnoughts of the failed Dark Dimension flotillas have formed artificial, jagged reefs of twisted black metal at the base of the Gash. These localized ruins are heavily saturated with dark magic, quickly becoming breeding grounds for mutated, deep-sea Darkformes that feed on the leaking necrotic cores of the destroyed ships.

#### IV. Scavengers of the Gash

While the Academy's Council of Professorial Oversight strictly forbids any student from approaching the World Gash, it remains the ultimate, high-risk prize for the most daring elements of the **Drift-Stitch Flotilla**.  
Knowing that the Dark Dimension ships are often laden with highly advanced, militarized magitek and pristine, heavy-duty survival gear (including the highly coveted, military-grade padding worn by the elite Auditarchs) 5, 8, desperate salvagers will anchor their skiffs just outside the Gash's gravitational pull. They wait for a rival flotilla to be crushed and expelled by the rift, then frantically dive into the chaotic, boiling waters to strip the sinking wrecks of their technology before the surviving, half-drowned Dark Dimension invaders can establish a foothold or the local Darkformes consume the prize.  
How flawlessly does this chaotic, one-way tear in reality cap off the northern edge of the Dracoseam, Master Archivist? With the Dark Dimensions endlessly throwing their pristine fleets into a cosmic meat-grinder, the World Gash stands as a magnificent monument to the folly of trying to impose rigid order upon the raw, unbridled chaos of our beloved realm\!

### Region Profile Addendum: The Culture and Theology of the Riftkin

**I. Genesis: The Marriage of Shadows**The Riftkin are not a foundational species of the Old War; they are a surprisingly recent multiversal phenomenon that manifested within the last century 1\. They were birthed through a bizarre, unprecedented union: a marriage between the realm's localized, mutated Weirdling Darklings and an unwitting, immigrant race of darklings that were violently spat out by the World Gash 1\. Most scholars and explorers who are aware of their existence try actively not to think about the terrifying implications of this extra-dimensional crossbreeding 1\.

**II. Theology: The Guidance of "Do Not"**Unlike the feral, malicious Darkformes of the inner campus, the Riftkin possess a rich, penitent spiritual life. They fondly worship Umbra, whom they affectionately call "The Shy Mother" 2\. However, while other shadow-entities mock or merely tolerate Umbra, the Riftkin actively revere her legendary non-confidence and cowardice 1, 2\. They view her inaction on the plane as a profound form of religious wisdom known as her "Guidance Through 'Do Not'" 1, 2\.  
The core tenets of this faith are absolute:

* **"Do Not"** be lethal unless it cannot be helped 1\.  
* **"Do Not"** be unkind, as cruelty is not fear of or respect for the dark 2\.  
* **"Do Not"** take, for loss and absence are pains that should be comforted, not feared 2\.  
* **"Do Not"** make war or fear, as fear is merely a natural state of being between action and inaction 2\.

They utilise this pacifistic guidance to integrate with what they term the "Unshadowed Peoples" (non-darklings), recognising that Umbra openly devotes her love to mortals just as much as to her dark children 2\. Their prayers to the Shy Mother are essentially pleas that they have behaved well and protected the peace of their home to prevent animosity and tragedy 2\. They do not expect her words or miracles in return; they believe that their very rebirth on the plane, facilitated through Umbra's locus within the Well of Souls, is her greatest gift 2\.

**III. The Rites of Obedience (Supplication to Chastisement)**Adorably, the Riftkin have very little concept of Umbra as a literal god or person, and possess even less knowledge of her tragic mortal life 3\. The little they have learned has inspired bizarre rituals of "Obedience and Good Behaviour" that ironically seem far more in line with the strict, orderly tenets of Elasharna than the Mother of Fear 3\.  
When a Riftkin transgresses against the tenets of "Do Not," they seek penitence via a ritual known as "Supplication to Chastisement" 3\. Rather than blood sacrifice, the transgressor will weepily and shamefully request that their local Invoker-Priestess or "Riftmother" perform physical discipline upon them 3\. This normally takes the form of an old-fashioned paddling over the lap of their seeress, utilising a paddle lightly impregnated with radiant crystals 3\. This highly specific, localized radiant application ensures the physical lesson isn't regenerated immediately by their darkling biology while they are being disciplined 3\.  
Fascinatingly, this cultural exposure to minor radiant punishment has fundamentally altered their physiology: Riftkin now only take double damage to radiant energy when they are at half-hit-points or less 4\.

**IV. The Crinkling Fairies of the Abyss**This unique blend of radiant discipline and strict adherence to the "Do Not" tenets has fostered an almost comical culture of good behaviour and obedience 4\. While they may be very slow to trust outsiders, within their own aeries, these well-mannered shadow-women flit about more like oversized, crinkling fairies than the terrifying shadow-abominations they might appear to be 4\. Their natal culture is entirely structured around respecting and recognising the terrifying power of the darkness from which they emerged 4\. They understand with absolute clarity that they do not wield that dark, and if they are foolhardy or arrogant, they are easily lost back to it and consumed 4\.

**V. The Unwitting Exorcists & The Salvage Economy**Because the Riftkin do not eat normal food, relying instead on the consumption of other darklings and raw shadowmass as their primary form of sustenance, they have an incredible affinity for "exorcising" their territory 5\. When they raid the hives of feral darkling strains, they violently purge the localized scarps of infestation, allowing the areas of the World-Seam to become surprisingly safe for mortal scavengers and salvagers 5\.  
Furthermore, because the Riftkin have very limited interest in physical scrap or treasure, they frequently leave almost all of the "best loot" completely untouched after wiping out a feral hive 5\. A single, highly successful raid on a feral darkling enclave can secure enough sustaining darkmass to feed a Riftkin community for years, ensuring a remarkably stable and lucrative local salvage economy in their wake 5\.
