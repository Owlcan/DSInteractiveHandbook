### Location Dossier: Colra’s Maw & The Stitch of the Jagged Sea

**Designation:** Timelocked Maritime Monument / Planar Seal**Location:** The Jagged Sea (South of the Faermeadows / Base of the Mighty Mountains)**Status:** Frozen / Latent Hazard / Extreme Hostile Biome**Key Entities Involved:** Colraneclast (The Glimmergale) & The Great Weaver

#### I. The Rupture of the 800s (The Calamity Level Event)

During the Era of Foundation, long before the apocalyptic bloodshed of the Old War, the demiplane’s ontological borders were still settling. The World-Seam—the literal, anti-curvature "crack" in dimensional reality where the substrate of timespace jarringly ends—was prone to violent fluctuations 1, 2\.  
Sometime in the 800s before present, a sudden, catastrophic "pop" occurred along the southern reaches of the World-Seam. This rupture caused a localized "Calamity Level Event," resulting in a massive, uncontrolled leak of raw, chaotic planar fluid and crushing oceanic pressure into the realm. If left unchecked, this torrential maelstrom threatened to completely submerge the southern coastline and destabilize the local climate.

#### II. The Vanity of the Glimmergale (The Top-Down Freeze)

The disaster occurred at the maritime borders of the Mighty Mountains, the sovereign territory of Colraneclast, the Glimmergale 3, 4\. As the Demiurge of Stasis, Colraneclast possessed an absurd, god-like control over the basic aspects of kinetics, motion, and ambient temperature 3, 4\.  
Viewing the chaotic leak not as a threat, but as a magnificent stage upon which to demonstrate her supremacy, Colraneclast descended upon the maelstrom. She did not seek to gently repair the planar boundary; she sought to subjugate it. Unleashing the absolute apex of her cryonic fury, she flash-froze the entire continent-sized whirlpool. The towering, violent waves and churning currents were instantly locked into a permanent, descending spiral of solid ice and sparkling, razor-sharp crystal. The frozen vortex became her ultimate Vanity Project—a breathtaking, terrifying monument designed to preserve the static cycle of ice in her domain and prove to all other flights that her power over kinetic cessation was absolute.

#### III. The Secret Stitch & The Unwitting Concert

Colraneclast’s vanity, however, was fundamentally flawed. Her cryonic stasis only "half-fixed" the anomaly. While the surface was a perfect, frozen sculpture, the terrifying, anti-reality kinetic friction of the World-Seam continued to violently grind against the underside of the ice 5\. Given enough centuries, the localized pressure would have inevitably shattered the Demiurge's frozen monument from beneath.  
The true salvation of the southern coast came from a completely different, entirely silent predator: the Great Weaver 6\. Sensing the errant, chaotic water threatening to leak through the bedrock and into her subterranean domain, the massive arachnoid matriarch descended into the abyssal trenches directly beneath the frozen whirlpool 6, 7\. Suspended upside down in the crushing dark, she utilized her colossal spinnerets and serrated chitinous limbs to literally "sew" the underside of the planar tear shut, weaving thick, indestructible cables of crystalline silk into the very bedrock of the seafloor to create an immaculate, watertight, metaphysical lattice 7\.  
This monumental feat of geographic preservation remains one of the realm's greatest secrets. Colraneclast and the Great Weaver never communicated, nor did they work in concert. Colraneclast froze the surface out of supreme, draconic ego; the Great Weaver stitched the bottom out of ravenous, territorial necessity to protect her webbed domain. Together, guided only by their respective, unyielding natures, they successfully ended the micro-crisis of the 800s. The result is Colra's Maw: a structure whose breathtaking beauty belongs to a dragon, but whose unbreakable, everlasting permanence belongs to a spider.

#### IV. The Maw Today: Ecologies and Pilgrims of the Frozen Vortex

Today, Colra's Maw is a harsh, static region of turbulent ice, flash-frozen water, and relentless snow. Yet, despite the extreme temperatures and razor-sharp crystalline terrain, it is a highly active ecosystem. It serves as a hunting ground for specialized darkformes, and a grueling pilgrimage site for the most resilient demographics of the Scholia Diaspros.

##### The Bio-Arcane Threats (Revised Taxa)

The dark ecology here has violently adapted to the cryonic stasis and the subterranean silk that bind the Jagged Sea, diverging significantly from their standard oceanic cousins.

* **The Darkforme Blackdeep Shark (The Scuttling Ice-Terror):** A twisted, hyper-chilled evolution of the standard Darkforme-Shark 1\. While its lesser cousins utilize their grotesque, shadowy crab-like legs to transition to muddy shores, the Blackdeep Shark has mutated to master the frozen surface of the Maw. It swims in the supercooled waters beneath the ice before bursting upward, its hardened, obsidian-glass crab-legs acting as brutal ice-picks to effortlessly scale the vertical, flash-frozen waves. The deafening, screeching sound of its claws tearing into the ice, combined with the sheer horror of witnessing its sudden terrestrial transition, forces unwary explorers into a DC 14 Constitution saving throw; on a failure, they suffer a humiliating "brown note incident" and lose their reactions, freezing them in place before the beast's ravenous bite strikes 1\.  
* **The Darkforme Benthic Leviathan (The Trapped Behemoth):** A localized, apocalyptic mutation of the standard Abyssal Leviathan 2\. This ancient, gargantuan dread prowls the benthic trenches directly beneath the flash-frozen surface of the whirlpool, trapped between Colraneclast's ice above and the Great Weaver's silk below. Capable of breathing both air and water, this colossal monstrosity exudes a terrifying, hyper-pressurized aura that turns the surrounding water into difficult terrain and inflicts both cold and necrotic damage to anything within 30 feet 4\. Because it deals double damage to objects and structures as a siege monster 4, the constant, booming cracks heard echoing through the ice of the Maw are often the Benthic Leviathan attempting—and failing—to smash its way up through the Demiurge's unbreakable timelocked surface.  
* **The Darkforme-Rime-Spinner (The Weaver's Echo):** A localized, unique abomination born from the ambient void-magic interacting with the Great Weaver's crystalline silk cables beneath the sea floor. This massive, arachnoid shadow-entity stalks the underside of the frozen waves, spinning invisible, absolute-zero tripwires between the jagged ice-spires above the surface. Victims caught in its freezing webs are slowly drained of their thermal energy before the spinner descends to claim them.


##### The Pilgrims and Explorers (Denizens of the Maw)

The sheer, apocalyptic danger of the Maw attracts specific student demographics from the Academy, driven by ancient bloodlines and primal instincts to conquer the frozen wastes.

* **Scions of the Glimmergale (The True Half Dragons):** The True Half Dragons of the Glacial Line of Colra trace their lineage directly back to the Glimmergale herself 8, 9\. For these towering, stoic students, the Maw is not a hazard—it is a holy site and a monument to their forebear. Thanks to their innate "Ice Walk" feature, they can stride across the difficult, slippery terrain of the frozen vortex without expending extra movement, and they possess a distinct advantage on saving throws to avoid falling prone on the slick, razor-sharp ice 10, 11\. Protected by their natural resistance and immunity to cold damage 9, these draconic scions frequently make harrowing pilgrimages to the center of the whirlpool. Here, they meditate upon the crushing fury of their ancestor, practicing their terrifying Frost Breath Weapons 9, 12 and their full Draconic Transformations 10, 11 in the absolute-zero winds.  
* **Deep-Sea Thrill-Seekers (The Carcharodonians):** The Carcharodonians are a formidable race of shark-folk perfectly adapted to life beneath the waves 13, 14\. Driven by primal instincts and a strong sense of self-preservation that often leans toward chaotic alignments 15, 16, the most daring Carcharodonian students view the Maw as the ultimate test of their aquatic supremacy. They dive into the freezing, kinetic-sheared waters at the very edges of the ice sheet to hunt the Darkforme-Sharks that mock their form 3, 13\. Their ability to navigate complex social and oceanic currents makes them the only reliable guides for Academy expeditions seeking to map the abyssal horrors trapped beneath the ice 14, 17\.  
* **Void-Frost Barnacles (Resource Foragers):** Clinging to the sheer, vertical faces of the highest frozen waves are clusters of Void-Frost Barnacles. Academy Alchemists and daring rogues brave the scuttling Darkforme-Sharks to harvest these scarce bioformes, which thrive on the trace amounts of chaotic planar fluid seeping from the World-Seam. When refined, they produce potent localized stasis-dust.
