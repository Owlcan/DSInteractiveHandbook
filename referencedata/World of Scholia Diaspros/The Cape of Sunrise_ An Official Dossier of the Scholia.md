### Region Profile: The Cape of Sunrise

**Designation:** Elite Coastal Sanctuary / Semi-Tropical Wilderness**Location:** Situated Northeast of the Lissome Plains, extending East of the Bay of Blitz.**Atmosphere:** Idyllic, tranquil, and sun-drenched, carrying the scent of sea salt, blooming hibiscus, and ancient pine.**Status:** Protected Wild Sanctuary (Decree of Hootz & Thorn-of-Sunrise)

#### I. Geography & Urban Development

Named in honor of the legendary early graduate and hero, Thorn-of-Sunrise 6, 7, the Cape of Sunrise is a breathtaking juxtaposition of high-society luxury and fiercely protected, untamed nature. The southern border bleeds seamlessly from the temporal grasses of the Lissome Plains 1 into semi-stable, lush transitional fields, before giving way to a copious, abundant semi-tropical forest that covers the bulk of the cape.  
By ancient, unyielding decree of Headmaster Horace Hootz 5 and Thorn-of-Sunrise herself, the vast majority of this forested interior is classified as a **Wild Sanctuary**. This edict has been fiercely respected for generations, ensuring that the rampant industrialization seen in places like Elzbereth or Darvitch never touches these sacred groves.

* **The Township of Solee:** Located on the northwestern side of the Cape, overlooking the sea. Solee is the largest common township in the region, boasting a population roughly equivalent to the settlements along the Road to Majica 11\. It features a sprawling, unique cliff and sea-side layout. Because it wraps around the natural topography, it creates a bizarre but charming urban flow: one moment you are walking through a quiet, almost rural seaside neighborhood, and just on the other side of the cliff face, you are thrust into a bustling, vibrant metropolitan center.  
* **The Sunrise Coast & Towerminium Blocks:** The eastern and southern coastlines are the ultimate winter destination for the realm's elite. Towering, elegant "Towerminium" blocks of high-end apartments and opulent winter homes line the most affluent stretches of the inhabited islands.  
* **The Crystal Archipelago & Serenity Reef:** Just off the coast lie two of the most famous and universally beloved resorts and beaches on the entire plane, boasting crystal-clear waters, flawless white sand, and perfect surfing waves that draw vacationing students and faculty year-round.

#### II. Demographics

The Cape of Sunrise is a melting pot of those who seek the sun, the sea, and the deep green.

* **The Beachcombers:** Humans and **Carcharodonians** (Shark-folk) 8 dominate the coastal regions and the Crystal Archipelago, running the resorts and managing the maritime trade.  
* **The Canopy Dwellers:** The **Seimei** (Floralfolk and Treebearers) 10 and the "Flighter" subraces of the **Crinkly-Crawlies** (Moths, Bees, Wasps, and Fireflies) manage the delicate ecosystem of the Wild Sanctuary, building their homes high in the semi-tropical canopy.  
* **The Groundkeepers:** Towering **Scampersaurians** roam the semi-stable southern plains, while highly insular, mystic tribes of **Kittenfolk** 9 and Rabbit-folk (**Bunbunkin**) maintain deep-wood enclaves, practicing ancient, nature-based magics far from the noise of Solee.

#### III. The Wild Sanctuary (Extant Fauna)

Because the interior is a fiercely protected sanctuary, the mundane and magical fauna here have flourished into highly formidable, unique taxa, completely separate from the corrupted Darkformes.

* **The Wizenbears:** Small, incredibly stout brown-black bears easily identified by a distinct, conical coif of hair on their heads. Do not let their small, cuddly stature fool you; Wizenbears possess potent, latent druidic magics and will cast localized entanglement or slumber spells if their territory is threatened.  
* **The Age-Hounds:** A tragic but fascinating species. Originally bred as an attempt to create magical service dogs to aid students in "littlespace," the magic mutated. These hounds now temporarily "eat" and gain the age of those they attack. When they bite, they drain the victim's maturity—often stripping them of their potty-training and physical coordination—and use that stolen age to temporarily drastically increase their own physical power and size.  
* **The Ground-Owl:** The most interesting minor fauna of the dense bramble thickets. It is a flightless, cagey, and incredibly resilient ground-dwelling avian. While it looks almost identical to a completely normal owl, its bones are dense and heavy, making it far stronger than any avian its size. Its feathers are perfectly camouflaged, allowing it to be a lethal, silent ambush predator during both the day and night.

#### IV. The Dark Ecology (The Cunning Shadows)

While the Cape is tranquil, it is not bereft of the plane's inherent darkness. Sharing borders with the Lissome Plains and the Greensea Expanse 3, the Cape inherits some of their darklings, but the unique, deep-wood environment has forced these entities to evolve into far more intelligent, cunning, and terrifying forms.

* **The Brush Patrols:** The mid-brush and less-dense forests are actively patrolled by **Jungle Buns** and **Darkling Lagoforms**—agile, shadowy rabbit-entities that use the thick undergrowth to evade the heavy strikes of the Scampersaurians.  
* **The Bushelgreeds:** Lurking in the tree boughs are the **Darkling Bushelgreeds**. These squirreloid abominations possess long, thick, slimy darkling-ooze tails, which they use as gelatinous storage sacks to hoard stolen nuts, shiny resort treasures, and occasionally, the skulls of unwary travelers.  
* **The Augur-Eyes:** Floating silently through the deepest groves are the Augur-Eyes. These terrifying entities are comprised of pure-black, floating eyes that radiate a hollow, hot gold light, seething with hateful, judgmental magic. Floating detached beside each eye is a disembodied, gnashing mouth that constantly masticates the ambient magic and dread of its prey, though it never actually eats physical meat.

**The Coven of the Wraiths:**To survive the formidable magical fauna like the Wizenbears and Age-Hounds, the apex Darklings of the Cape chose the route of high intellect and dark devotion. Deep within the groves reside the **Darkling Wraith Seeresses**.  
These are not mindless oozes. They are gorgeous, terrifying shades composed of long-dead-but-never-forgotten sorrows. They appear as beautiful, weeping spectral women wrapped in tattered, shadowy finery, proudly and undeniably wearing the spectral manifestation of **gorgeous, ancient-styled thick diapers** that glow with a cursed, melancholic light. These highly intelligent Seeresses maintain hidden, dark altars dedicated to **Umbra** (The Veiled Mother of Fear) 4, 12\. They spend their eternal existence weeping and lamenting the coward-goddess's absolute refusal to heed their dark pleas. Operating in organized covens of great import, these diapered wraiths are masterminds who actively steal important magical artifacts from the resorts in Solee, utilizing them in terrifying attempts to initiate dark sacrifices to force Umbra's hand.
