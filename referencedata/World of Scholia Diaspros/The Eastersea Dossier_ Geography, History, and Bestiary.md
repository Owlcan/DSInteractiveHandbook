

### Region Profile: The Eastersea

**Designation:** Uncharted Pelagic Territory / Volatile Boundary Zone**Location:** Situated East of the Faermeadows, extending to the absolute dimensional edge of the plane.**Atmosphere:** Breathtakingly beautiful, violently chaotic, and dense with oceanic magic.**Key Phenomenon:** The Dracoseam (The Eastern World-Seam)

#### I. Geography & The Building Age Origins

The Eastersea is a vast, sparkling ocean that rolls gently away from the eastern border of the Faermeadows. However, this magnificent body of water is not a naturally occurring phenomenon; it is a masterpiece of deliberate, titanic planar architecture.  
During the Founding Era, Headmaster Horace Hootz sought to stabilize the boundaries of the demiplane 1\. To forge the eastern oceans, he collaborated directly with one of the most powerful entities to ever grace the realm: **Almadarnellee, The Sea Lady** 2\. In her Regal Countenance, Almadarnellee was a massive, impossibly long serpentine sea-dragon boasting eighteen sets of wings and supreme aquatic magic 2\. Together, Hootz and the Sea Lady carved the massive abyssal trenches and utilized her physical movements to localized the manipulation of the seas, filling the basin with pristine, thriving water 2\.

#### II. The Dracoseam & The Chaotic Climate

As one sails further east, the serene, domesticated magic of the fae-shores gives way to a violently turbulent and magically dense seascape. This drastic climatic shift is dictated by the region's proximity to the eastern edge of the World-Seam.  
In the Eastersea, this planar birth-scar takes a highly distinct, terrifyingly beautiful shape: **The Dracoseam**. Viewed from afar, the jagged, glowing tear in reality perfectly resembles a colossal, diving dragon etched into the sky and the sea. The sheer kinetic friction of the Academy's reality scraping against the absolute void of the Dracoseam generates massive atmospheric pressure changes and raw magical fallout. As a result, the climate of the deep Eastersea is fiercely chaotic: sudden, localized arcane typhoons, gravity-inverted waterspouts, and prismatic auroras that set the night sky ablaze.

#### III. Recent History & Current Events

The pristine waters built by Hootz and Almadarnellee were forever scarred during the climax of the Old War.  
**The Tragedy of the East Sea (\~701 Years Ago):** Recognizing Almadarnellee's refusal to slaughter the innocent, Sorceress Tabitha Caelestis dealt a stunningly cruel punishment 5\. She pressurized a subterranean cavern into a massive injector of concentrated Darkforme animus, polluting the entire East Sea with pure, metaphysical malice 5\. To save the oceans from Tabitha's wicked spite, Almadarnellee consumed the entire body of pollution herself 3\. The sheer volume of corruption broke the Sea Lady, twisting her into **The Ebongoreel (Dark She of the Sea)**, a multitudinously lengthy, monstrous Darkforme that still haunts the deep waters 3\.  
**The Contemporary Crisis:** In the modern era, the Eastersea is experiencing profound instability. Following the "Great Reawakening of Magic" (\~490 Years Ago), the ambient magical energies across the plane intensified 6\. This surge supercharged the Dracoseam, causing the dimensional tear to "bleed" raw, unfiltered mana into the water at an unprecedented rate. The local taxa—specifically the Sorcerous Whales—are currently working in a state of perpetual exhaustion to filter this excess magic, while the Carcharodonians desperately patrol the deep trenches to keep the mutated Void-Orcas from overwhelming the strained ecosystem.

#### IV. Demographics: The Pelagic Nomads

The Eastersea is far too volatile for permanent, static cities, demanding a population that is fluid and adaptable.

* **The Waves Folk Carcharodonians:** The undisputed masters of the Eastersea are the Waves Folk. This nomadic subrace of Shark-folk is perfectly suited to roaming the open oceans and chaotic coastal areas.  
* **The Sea Elves:** Operating in deep-water, magically stabilized coral pavilions are the Sea Elves, maintaining a symbiotic, trade-based relationship with the Waves Folk.

### V. Taxa of the Eastersea

The ambient, chaotic magic bleeding off the Dracoseam has supercharged the marine biology of the Eastersea, creating awe-inspiring extant taxa and terrifying, shadow-infused predators perfectly adapted to the high-mana environment.
