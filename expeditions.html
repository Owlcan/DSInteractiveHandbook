Scholia Diaspros Expedition System

Step 1: Character Preparedness

Welcome, adventurer! To begin your expedition, please enter your character's relevant skill bonuses and ability scores.

Your character will be considered *indisposed* for the period of the Expedition*. They cannot be used for other activities, such as dungeons, during this time.

Note: Herbalism is considered a *Knowledge* skill for the purposes of this Expedition- and proficiency is granted by those with proficiency with the Herbalism kit and its related skills.

Step 2: Choose Your Expedition Type

Expeditions fall into one of two categories. Select the type that best suits your goals:

Expanded Reconnaissance

This is the "getting out and seeing the world" part of expeditions. By undertaking these expeditions, a character will map a chosen region, putting up beacons and tracking devices. This unlocks more specific locations for future expeditions and other activities.

Speculation

For those who are after more than the sights, Speculative Expeditions focus on securing locations, resources, and items either for one's own needs or at the behest of officials attendant to the functions of the Realm. These expeditions reward tangible resources and reward items based on survivalist and combat performance.

Step 3: Choose Your Zone & Goals (Magical Plains)

You have selected the **Magical Plains** for your expedition. Now, choose a specific goal.

Required Checks, Suggested Skills, and Valuable Tools

Throughout your expedition, you may face challenges requiring specific skills or tools:

  • *Adventuring Kits, Climbing Packs,* and *Cartography Tools* are essential for overcoming most lack of training in the **Survival** skill.
  • Similarly, *knowledge of **Nature*** replaces the need for Survival skills in certain circumstances, such as locating flora and fauna and recognizing them.
  • *Local knowledge* can also aide in figuring out if you are in the right location for any given encounter or desired materials/creatures/sites.
