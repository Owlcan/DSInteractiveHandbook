## **Part I: The Coastal Horrors & Deep Pelagic Terrors**

### **1\. Corsair-Orca**

*Large Abomination, Chaotic Evil*

The dreaded raider of the deep Eastersea, the Corsair-Orca is a highly intelligent, anthropomorphic cetacean made of semi-solid shadow condensate. They wear hats and coats fashioned from tattered black sails and kelp, wielding cutlasses of hardened dark-ice.

**Region:** Derived from *The Eastersea Dossier: Geography, History, and Bestiary*. They terrorize the trade lanes between Darvitch and the outer islands.

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)

**Hit Points** 95 (10d10 \+ 40\)

**Speed** 10ft., swim 60ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Str \+8, Con \+7

**Skills** Athletics \+8, Intimidation \+4, Stealth \+5

**Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant

**Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic

**Condition Immunities** Cursed

**Senses** Blindsight 120ft., Passive Perception 12

**Languages** Aquan, Abyssal

**Challenge** 5 (1,800XP)

**Traits:**

* **Shadow Amphibiousness:** The Corsair-Orca can breathe both air and water. While in water, it can take the Hide action as a bonus action.  
* **Keen Hearing:** The Corsair-Orca has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.  
* **Echolocation Suppression:** While in pitch darkness or heavily obscured water, the Corsair-Orca can emit a magical sonar click. Up to three creatures of its choice within 60ft. must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have their passive perception reduced by \-5 for 1minute.

**Actions:**

* **Multiattack:** The Corsair-Orca makes two attacks: one with its Hardened Dark-Ice Cutlass and one with its Crushing Tail.  
* **Hardened Dark-Ice Cutlass:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* \+8 to hit, reach 5ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 \+ 5\) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) cold damage.  
* **Crushing Tail:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* \+8 to hit, reach 10ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 \+ 5\) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
* **Harpoon of the Deep (Recharge** 5-6**):** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* \+5 to hit, range 30/90ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 \+ 2\) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 16\) and pulled up to 30ft. directly toward the Corsair-Orca.

### **2\. Slip-Stream Seal**

*Medium Beast, Playful Neutral*

The beloved, friction-defying companions of the Waves Folk. These seals are born from pure elemental water and light shadow-mist, allowing them to glide over ice and oceans without a single drop of drag.

**Region:** Derived from *The Eastersea Dossier: Geography, History, and Bestiary*. They inhabit the pristine open waters and coastal shallows of the Eastersea.

**Armor Class** 14 (Natural Agility)

**Hit Points** 36 (8d8)

**Speed** 10ft., swim 60ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills** Acrobatics \+6, Perception \+4

**Damage Resistances** Cold

**Senses** Darkvision 60ft., Passive Perception 14

**Languages** Understands Aquan but cannot speak

**Challenge** 1 (200XP)

**Traits:**

* **Zero-Friction Swimmer:** The Slip-Stream Seal possesses innate magic that reduces water friction to absolute zero. It ignores difficult terrain caused by water currents, rapids, or aquatic spells, and opportunity attacks against it are made with disadvantage while it is swimming.  
* **Friendly Boop:** The seal intentionally seeks out friendly humanoids. Its playful nature grants it advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to interact favorably with other creatures.

**Actions:**

* **Playful Ram:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* \+6 to hit, reach 5ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 \+ 4\) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone by the seal's frictionless slide.  
* **Slip-Stream Splash (Recharge** 4-5**):** The seal splashes water at a creature within 15ft.. The target must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the target's feet or boots are coated in frictionless water, halving their movement speed and giving them disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws until the start of the seal's next turn.

While grappled, the target is restrained, and the Trench-Walker can use a bonus action to reel the target up to 20ft. directly toward it.
