### Location Dossier: The Glacial Domain (The Mighty Mountains)

**Designation:** Sovereign Draconic Territory / Extreme Hostile Biome**Location:** A polar range situated south of the Three Sisters, bordering the Jagged Sea 1, 2\.**Status:** Causally Chilled / Active Pilgrimage Site**Key Entity:** Colraneclast (The Glimmergale), The Demiurge of Stasis 1, 3\.

#### I. Topography & The Absolute Zero

The Mighty Mountains are an unforgiving, hyper-lethal polar range characterized by blinding blizzards and sheer, icy cliffs 1, 4\. The atmosphere here is thin and razor-sharp, heavily influenced by the lingering ambient magic of its ancient mistress. Before her fatal assault on the Academy, Colraneclast utilized her god-like control over kinetics, motion, and ambient temperature to lock the peaks in a state of eternal, crystalline stasis 3\. The glaciers do not flow; they are suspended like jagged glass teeth against the bruised-purple sky. To traverse the Mighty Mountains without specialized absolute-zero survival gear and high-bulk thermal padding is to invite instantaneous, deep-tissue frostbite.

#### II. The Throne of the Glimmergale

Before she descended to the Jagged Sea to flash-freeze the continental whirlpool known as Colra's Maw, Colraneclast ruled as the undisputed Mistress of the Mighty Mountains 1, 3\. Her majestic roosts and the frozen aeries of her loyal flights still dot the highest plateaus. These colossal ice-caverns and frozen spires are treated as sacred ground. They are perfectly preserved monuments to her howling, silver-blue draconic majesty, functioning as open-air temples where the whipping winds simulate her devastating sonic fury 1, 3\.

#### III. The Pilgrimage of the Glacial Line

Today, the Mighty Mountains are the spiritual and ancestral homeland for the True Half Dragons of the Glacial Line of Colra 4\. These stoic, towering scions carry the freezing, silver-blue blood of the ancient ice-drakes and the heavy, heartbreaking legacy of the devoured Demiurge 3, 4\.

* **The Ascent:** Possessing a highly specialized climbing speed, these half-dragons effortlessly scale the sheer, icy cliffs of the range 4\.  
* **The Cryonic Crucible:** Blessed with innate cryonic fortitude, they can invoke complete immunity to cold damage, allowing them to shrug off the lethal blizzards that would freeze lesser beings solid 5\. They journey to the highest peaks to meditate upon the crushing fury of their ancestor, practicing their terrifying Frost Breath Weapons and their full draconic transformations within the howling, absolute-zero winds 4, 6\.

#### IV. The Dark Ecology of the Peaks

Because the environment is so exceptionally hostile, the dark ecology that survives here has violently adapted to harness the cold. The Mighty Mountains are the hunting grounds for one of the most lethal high-altitude abominations in our bestiary:

* **The Darkforme-Rime-Stalker (The Avalanche Cat):** Descending from the snow-choked high altitudes to hunt along the mountain passes and the adjacent frozen shores of the Maw, these sleek, feline-like predators are perfectly camouflaged. Composed entirely of smoking black shadow-stuff but heavily armored in overlapping plates of jagged, frozen obsidian shadow-glass, they stalk their prey through the whiteout conditions. Their shadow-claws drain the heat directly from a victim's body, utilizing the ambient freezing temperatures to secure a rapid, silent kill.
