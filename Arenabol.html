Pre-game checklist for predefined opponents
  1. Each player builds their own team on their chosen side (Red home / Blue away).
  2. Each player exports their team to a file.
  3. To face a specific opponent, import their team file into the agreed color slot (Red or Blue).
  4. When everyone is happy, hit INITIALIZE MATCH to start.

Decide out-of-game which side goes first; the sim will roll initiative when the match starts, and both copies should follow that result to avoid desync.

Races & Roles Overview

Quick reference for all available races and the five core roles. This is meant as a table-read cheat sheet before you lock in teams.

Striker

Primary scorer. High DEX, best at shooting. Wants the ball near goal.

Range: Pass 5, shots scale by distance; loves clean lanes.

Midfielder

Connector players. Good at passes and support, living around midfield.

Range: Pass 5, good tackle footprint.

Defender

Thick front line. STR‑focused, taxes shots and tackles near your goal.

Threat: Big threat radius; best at clogging lanes.

Goalie

Anchors the 5‑point gate. Great at denying close shots and cleaning up.

Position: Starts in the inner 5‑point zone.

Flex

Weird wizard. INT‑leaning support with small spell list instead of raw stats.

Toolkit: Soft buffs, debuffs, and diaper tricks.

Race Cards

Stats are base modifiers on top of 10s; speed is feet per turn. Each card reserves a strip for future race art.

Core Rules Snapshot

This is the quick‑reference version of Aether‑Padded Punt. Use it to remind yourself what the buttons mean.

Objective & Scoring

  • Score by shooting the ball into the opponent's goal zones on the far side.
  • Outer band is worth 1 point, middle is 3, inner glowing gate is 5.
  • Ball starts at midfield; first initiative is decided by a d20 roll‑off.

Turn Flow

  • Only the team with initiative can act; each turn is one team doing all their moves/actions.
  • Select a player to see their current move speed for this turn.
  • Move by clicking tiles; each player typically gets one action (pass, shoot, tackle, punt, foul, racial, or ultimate).
  • After you're done with all your toys, hit END TURN to hand initiative over.

Actions (Buttons)

  • PASS: Throw the ball 5 tiles forward; nearby foes may impose threat or intercept.
  • SHOOT: Fire toward the goal; DC scales by distance and defender pressure.
  • TACKLE: Hit an adjacent foe; on success you snare or, on a 20, root them.
  • PUNT: Diaper‑ass punt that sends the ball far downfield if the STR check holds up.
  • FOUL: Risky hit that deals HP damage and hands out yellow flags.
  • RACE / ULTIMATE: Once‑per‑half racial powers and a three‑star ultimate.

Statuses & Penalties

  • Rooted: Can't move at all.
  • Snared: Speed reduced and accuracy slightly worse.
  • KO: Out for a few turns; drops the ball if carrying.
  • Penalty Box: Three yellow flags earns you a timeout.
  • Diaper States: Full diapers impose attack/move penalties unless mitigated.

Halves & Reset

  • The engine auto‑advances to the second half after enough turns; some per‑half abilities refresh.
  • Red stars earned by big plays charge your ultimate; three stars lights it up.

Copy‑Paste Play‑By‑Post

  • COPY grabs a compact JSON snapshot of the current board.
  • Your opponent pastes it into their copy via PASTE / MERGE or the setup textarea.
  • If the seeds match, only positions, scores, and state update and you'll see pieces slide into place.
  • Goals scored on the remote side's turn pop little fireworks by the appropriate gate.
